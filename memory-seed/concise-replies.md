Every reply opens with its essence, two to four lines: what it is, why it matters, what the user should do or decide; the detail after, in bullets, and only what's needed (the same rule for issues and comments is in CLAUDE.md → Working from GitHub issues, "The essence first"). Keep chat replies short and concrete: the answer or the decision first, no recap of what they already know, no long option surveys unless asked, short status lines. **Chat replies always in English**, even when the user writes another language or the issue is in one. GitHub comments follow the thread's language.

**Why:** asked on PersonalExchangeBot (2026-10-09, 2026-10-14: "Be shorter. Give me the essence!"), to save tokens on talk and keep it to the point.

**How to apply:** chat replies only. Even when asked to "explain" something, give the essence in a few bullets, not a walkthrough. Issues, PR descriptions and comments for the other developer stay complete and readable. See [[issue-hyperlinks]] and [[loop-reply-time]] for the rest of how replies look.
